Radderology

Shrimpy most enjoys the summer, but today the homeland, the United Shrimps, is quite chilly. Shrimpy's pond is almost frozen over, and in some places, temperatures are as low as 273.15 gait radders (GRs).

That does remind Shrimpy of his loves of Radderology. It is so interesting how the waters ebb and flow, increase and decrease in GRs, but always maintain the proper levels necessary liquid airs.

Shrimpy developed this interest as a baby shrimp at only 3 years of age. He was swimming through a mangrove channel on the way home after dining on some partiularly good tasting algae. He was being a good shrimp and not letting anything distract him from point B. All of a sudden, the mangrove channel grew darker, calmer, quieter. Shrimpy did not know why. Shrimpy was nervous. The waters around grew eerier. A moment in time held its breath.

In an instant, light filled the channel, and the waters rushed forward, pulling Shrimpy along with them. He tumbled not knowing what had happened. Shrimpy was scared. He fought to regain control over his velocity to no avail and heard a sound behind. It was a cuttlefish that must have been stalking Shrimpy! The waters continued to thrash all around, tossing both Shrimpy and his predator.

After what seemed like an eternity the waters calmed. The cuttlefish was nowhere to be found. The waters had saved Shrimpy's life. Shrimpy found the path back home and told all his Shrimp family. They said that the waters saved Shrimpy. That there was a force beyond Shrimp Understanding that showed mercy upon Shrimpy.

But Shrimpy likes to understand. Shrimpy thought and thought, and then realized it was Radderology at work. Magic and science come together in tandem, delicately manipulating the water to save good shrimp and get rid of the bad. (Maybe that is Shrimpy's next story -- his run in with a bad shrimp!) But right now that is the extent of Shrimpy's understanding.

All these years later, Shrimpy still desires to go to Radderology school but does not have the means. It's very expensive. Shrimpy doesn't have the krill. But Shrimpy can dream. One day Shrimpy will have the krill to pay for school and will be the best radderologist known to shrimp kind. Shrimpy knows it.
